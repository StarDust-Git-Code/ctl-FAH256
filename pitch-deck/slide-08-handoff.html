The Handoff Protocol

1

Outgoing Driver Scans

Driver A opens Cargo Services App, selects "Handoff", scans cargo QR code. App verifies active shipment.

2

Outgoing Confirms Release

App shows current temp, tamper status, integrity. Driver A confirms "Release Custody" with biometric/PIN.

3

Incoming Driver Scans

Driver B scans same QR code. App prompts "Accept Custody". Shows live data for verification.

4

Incoming Confirms Acceptance

Driver B confirms with biometric/PIN. GPS, timestamp, both driver IDs locked. Backend records handoff event.
