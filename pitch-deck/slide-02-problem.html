The Problem

Why cold-chain logistics is broken — and why it matters

Temperature Excursions

WHO estimates 50% of vaccines are wasted globally due to temperature control failures. A single excursion can render an entire shipment unsafe — with no verifiable proof of when or where it happened.

Falsified Records

Manual paper logs and disconnected digital systems allow temperature records to be altered, backdated, or fabricated — with no cryptographic proof of authenticity.

Tampering & Theft

Cargo boxes can be opened, contents swapped, or devices disabled during transit. Without tamper-evident sensing, there's no real-time alert — and no forensic trail after the fact.

Broken Chain of Custody

Driver handoffs are informal — verbal, unrecorded, or paper-signed. When incidents occur, no one knows who had custody, where the transfer happened, or whether protocols were followed.
